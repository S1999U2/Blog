1. [Bronze Age iron artifacts likely originated from meteorites, not from smelting terrestrial iron 🏆☄️](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0305440317301322). A geochemical approach using Fe:Co:Ni ratios can distinguish terrestrial from extraterrestrial iron. Modern iron and iron ores have distinct Fe:Co:Ni ratios compared to those found in Bronze Age artifacts.

2. [The U.S. never adopted the metric system due to Caribbean pirates 📐🏴‍☠️.](https://www.npr.org/sections/thetwo-way/2017/12/28/574044232/how-pirates-of-the-caribbean-hijacked-americas-metric-system)
	When the U.S. was considering standardizing measurements, Thomas Jefferson arranged for French scientist Joseph Dombey to deliver a kilogram standard. British privateers intercepted Dombey in the Caribbean, leading to his capture and eventual death. The kilogram standard reached the U.S. too late to influence the metric system’s adoption.

9. [The ancient Phoenician city of Byblos is the origin of the terms *book* and *bible* 📖.](https://web.stanford.edu/class/complit172c/8jan.html#:~:text=Byblos%20was%20a%20Phoenician%20city,biblion%20%2D%2D%20'little%20scroll'.)

23. [Earthquakes on the U.S. East Coast are felt farther than ones on the West Coast](https://bigthink.com/strange-maps/earthquake-west-east-coast/)
	The reason? East of the Rocky Mountains, much of the rocky underground is hundreds of millions of years older than the subterranean formations to the west. Older rocks tend to be harder and denser, and older fault lines have had more time to “heal.”
	All of that means seismic waves travel more efficiently across the eastern U.S. The reverse is true in the West, where younger faults absorb more of the wave energy, so it doesn’t spread as far.
	This not only explains why a California quake is felt in a smaller area, but also why this more focused shaking can cause much more damage.

35. [Cats have vertical pupils because they help with low-light conditions and enable high precision in judging prey distance 🐈👁️](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VHh-EoaTevg)
	Vertical pupils can expand and contract over a very large range (up to ~135× area change), allowing effective vision from bright daylight to near darkness. They also enhance depth perception via blur gradients. Blur is preserved primarily along horizontal contours (e.g., the ground), leading to a strong gradient of defocus with distance, which small, ground-level predators can exploit to estimate prey distance.
